## Phase 1B decisions to preserve

1. **Current price applies to new contracts unless an administrator explicitly confirms a move.** A new revision and Stripe Price never rewrite an existing subscription's frozen 1A `config_revision`, `price_key`, or original amount. Existing subscriptions and unused pack lots remain intact across billing-model changes; they can fund new bundles under their original terms when the feature status permits use. The requested Phase 1 must include an explicit admin action to move selected subscribers. That action must use append-only contract-version rows, an active-version pointer, an immutable verified migration intent, current Stripe state, an explicit proration confirmation, and revision CAS. It must not update the original 1A audit row or silently migrate every subscriber when a price changes. Step 1 builds the database contract; its provider adapter and admin UI are a later Phase 1B packet.
2. **No provider object is a price quote by itself.** The immutable server reservation and the active 1A pricing revision determine payer, option, pack quantity, exact amount/currency, and provider Price. Browser input never chooses customer, payer, amount, revision, or access status.
3. **Stripe is the platform account.** Form Coach uses its own `form_coach` metadata/namespace and a dedicated Stripe Customer mapping per Form Coach payer. It is platform revenue: no Connect account context, `transfer_data`, `on_behalf_of`, application fee, Armory commission, or coach payout. Keep raw Stripe IDs and client secrets server-only.
4. **Billing lifecycle is durable and retryable.** Product/Price/customer/Checkout creates use stable idempotency keys and persisted reservations before network calls. Timeout or uncertain outcome remains `unknown` and is reconciled against stable metadata/provider IDs before retrying; never mint a second Product, Price, Customer, Checkout, receipt, trial, or credit merely because a response was lost. A stale config CAS cannot activate objects for a newer revision.
5. **Trial is not a paid receipt.** A zero-dollar invoice may be audited only as an invoice/trial fact after provider state and the one-time Form Coach trial claim are verified. Do not put it in the positive-amount Checkout receipt table, create a fake paid receipt, or mint pack credits from it. Time trials and finite-assessment trials are claimed once per feature/payer across pricing revisions and retain their original source/revision/invoice provenance. No seed or test auto-comps or grants a real account.
6. **Subscription changes use current provider state plus CAS.** Webhook event timestamps alone do not decide state. Reconcile the current subscription and exact feature/customer/payer/price binding before changing status or access. New paid invoice periods have an invoice-level unique audit; duplicate delivery cannot extend access or mint a credit twice.
7. **Missing mappings are not successful entitlement.** Persist unmatched/unknown events with the stable Stripe event identity and retry the same event after reconciliation. Return success only after the event is durably applied, definitively blocked, or durably queued for retry; if durable ownership was not recorded, return a retryable failure. Do not acknowledge an unmapped payment as complete and leave it ungranted forever.
8. **Feature-status rules are distinct from billing state.** `disabled` blocks all new sends and attempts, including previously funded ones; it retains read-only history, billing records, export, and immediate owner clip deletion. `pricing_pending` allows only explicit comp for new use and blocks new paid bundles; already-funded sent bundles remain usable through their due date while the feature is enabled. When status is live again, grandfathered subscriptions and unused pack lots can fund new bundles under the original contract. Cancellation, refund, expiry, zero balance, or a payer/config change does not erase immutable funding.
9. **Funding is frozen at assessment send.** The active payer/config revision is read when a draft is sent. Coach payer funding is recorded at send; member payer funding occurs at first assessed attempt. An unfunded member-payer bundle remains member-funded after an admin payer switch; a coach-funded bundle never charges its recipient. Active subscription, comp, or time-based trial records an immutable funding receipt without decrementing credits. A per-assessment charge or finite-assessment trial consumes exactly one unit. Reopening the same Needs Work bundle requires a separate coach-authorized attempt-window record, reuses its same funding receipt, and never charges again; it cannot mutate the receipt due date. A new charged generation requires an explicit coach action.
10. **Refunds are source-specific and debt-free.** A verified refund can remove only unused units from its originating pack lot. It cannot claw back funded bundle access while enabled, remove other lots/trials/subscription access, create negative credits, or trigger hidden recharge. The money-to-unit rule for partial monetary refunds is not defined in the approved contract. Initial handling should persist the refund for review and withhold automatic credit-unit changes unless it exactly matches the supported whole-pack refund policy; do not invent a prorating formula or silently revoke access.
11. **Account deletion is a persistent fence.** Check the existing deletion tombstone under the documented payer/deletion lock order before customer, Checkout, subscription, receipt, trial, or access mutations. Deletion must cancel exact known subscriptions and expire known sessions, reconcile uncertain creates, and remain pending until no unresolved external create can later grant access. A late event after tombstone is recorded and cancelled/blocked, never entitled. Keep financial evidence needed for reconciliation without retaining unnecessary profile data.
12. **Access/read boundaries are explicit.** A later private `has_feature_access`/assessment-read contract must derive access from current global status, authenticated actor, assessment recipient/coach relationship, immutable funding receipt, due date, and any separately authorized retry window. It must distinguish new-bundle pricing from existing sent-bundle access. A client cannot pass a receipt ID/pin/status to claim access or call a generic debit RPC. Only service-owned send/first-attempt RPCs may create funding atomically. Owner billing views expose the user's own summary, sanitized receipts/invoice history, and credit-lot balances; raw provider IDs stay server-side. An assessment recipient of a coach-funded bundle need not buy a personal entitlement. The normal account membership gate continues to govern new member feature use.

Proposed additive records and invariants:

- **Stripe sync saga:** append-only or state-transition-guarded jobs for a pricing revision's Product/Price objects. Persist operation identity, expected current revision, exact request digest, stable idempotency key, provider object IDs, and `reserved / unknown / bound / verified / failed` reconciliation state. A bound object keeps its provider ID while verification remains outstanding; it cannot transition to failed and discard that ID. Reuse across revisions is allowed only for the same immutable object kind, price key, and payload digest. Never expose arbitrary provider payload. Activate the pricing revision only with a fresh expected-revision CAS after every required Product/Price is reconciled. Checkout always uses the immutable Price ID stored on its selected revision/option; Product default price is not an entitlement or quote source.
- **Payer customer mapping:** unique `(feature_key, payer_id)` to one platform Customer ID, with immutable owner binding, deletion-fence checks, and customer-create saga state. Reuse that customer across pack sizes and revisions for the same payer; reject cross-payer reuse. Do not create a recipient customer for a coach-funded bundle.
- **Invoice audit and paid-through proof:** append-only unique Stripe invoice ID linked to the immutable subscription/contract, event, payer, period, currency, `amount_due`, and `amount_paid`. It records positive renewals once and verified zero-dollar trial invoices separately. Trial classification requires a payer-once trial claim and provider-confirmed trial state. It is not a credit receipt and cannot be used as one. Initial paid subscription checkout requires the exact positive Checkout receipt paired with a matching verified settled invoice and payment intent; the proof captures that invoice's immutable `period_end`, never a later raw subscription period. Receipt, invoice, and provider-state updates trigger reconciliation so both arrival orders converge. Later access is capped by verified paid-invoice or immutable trial-claim proof and current provider period end. A legacy `feature_trial_access_grants` row alone is not authorization. A provider period extension by itself cannot extend paid access, and a refund removes future access proof without rewriting funded assessment history.
- **Trial claim:** unique feature/payer claim across config revisions, with trial kind (`trial_days` or finite assessments), original config revision, source subscription/invoice if applicable, granted window or count, and immutable provenance. A retry of the same provider event returns the same result; changed identities are rejected.
- **Provider event retry:** durable per-event state distinguishing received, retryable-unmatched, applied, and terminally-blocked/mismatch. Stable event ID plus object identity is immutable. A same-event retry after exact customer/session/subscription binding or fresh current-state fetch may converge once; applied/blocked duplicates remain idempotent. A stale event cannot overwrite newer state.
- **Subscription contract migration:** add append-only contract versions, an active-version pointer, and verified migration-intent/state records in Step 1. The later admin action is required before Phase 1 is considered complete. Freeze the original 1A subscription row; require current admin authorization, explicit per-subscriber confirmation, explicit proration choice/confirmation, fresh provider state, provider verification of the new price, and CAS of both provider state and the active pointer. Price edits do not automatically move any subscriber.

All writes are service-only or narrow admin RPCs with role checks, safe `search_path`, RLS, immutable ledger triggers, unique constraints, and consistent lock ordering. Safe owner projections do not expose processor identifiers. Billing/trial/customer mutation functions check the persistent deletion fence; new bundle funding also checks current feature status. The final Step 1 migration and matching 155-assertion rollback-only TAP passed on approved clone G. The two-connection lock interleavings passed on H with retained synthetic fixtures and verified advisory-lock evidence. Source clone C and G retain history at 56 rows/latest `20261118151000`, because the clone test applied direct DDL without fabricating a migration-ledger entry. D, E, and F are preserved and must not be reused. These local checks do not cover provider adapters, UI, webhooks, shared deletion/export callsites, or hosted migration. Never apply this candidate to the source/default/hosted database.

## UI behavior and status copy requirements

- `pricing_pending`: explain that new paid Form Coach bundles are not available; an explicit comp remains possible only where an admin has recorded one. Preserve history and any existing immutable contracts, but do not let them fund new use until the status allows it.
- `disabled`: explain the global pause for all new sends/attempts, including funded bundles. Keep read-only results, billing records, export, and owner clip deletion available.
- Live but missing a usable verified price/sync: fail closed with a retry/support state; never display a stale price as purchasable.
- Customer/Checkout create unknown: show pending reconciliation and do not offer another Checkout for the same unresolved request.
- Payment not confirmed: display pending, never infer entitlement from the Checkout return URL.
- A member-payer first assessment attempt must verify/consume its frozen payment/credit/trial source atomically; a coach-payer bundle records funding at send and the recipient is not asked to pay again.
- Existing subscription changed price: show its recorded legacy price/contract and renewal state; active pricing controls future subscriptions only. Do not suggest an automatic migration.
- Billing portal and account export stay available to a signed-in user even if `requireAccount()` would reject feature use because platform membership expired. Clip privacy deletion stays separately owner-authorized and does not check feature status, credits, billing, or base entitlement.
