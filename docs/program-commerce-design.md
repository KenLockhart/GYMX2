## Scope

Programs will be purchasable only by signed-in accounts in the Programs area. Guest checkout remains Armory-only. A member buyer needs active Regular/Premium eligibility for both coach and platform-owned paid Programs; a coach-owned Program additionally requires that it is published and the buyer is linked to that coach. Enforce these requirements in the signed-in UI, paid RPCs, day access, and media signing, not only through layout auth. Own billing/receipt/cancellation routes remain available after membership expires. Preserve current free-program eligibility and daily-unlock behavior rather than globally broadening or restricting its RLS. Telehealth and medical-product purchasing are out of scope.

The buyer chooses one of two fixed-total payment choices:

- **Pay in full:** one charge for the effective total.
- **Monthly installments:** a finite number of charges that sum to the effective total. This is not an indefinitely renewing subscription.

Payment stages open only when the corresponding payment has settled. A lesson can never be exposed before its immutable ordinal stage is paid, and the final lesson stays locked until the effective discounted total is fully settled. Full payment or a fully funded 100% promotion removes the payment-stage cap, but the existing daily timeline still applies.

### Completion and deletion invariants

`program_day_completions` points to mutable day rows with cascading deletion, so it cannot remain the history store for pinned or purchased revisions. The versioned completion table must reference immutable lesson/version IDs and survive changes to `program_days` or deletion of the buyer account. Preserve the old completion path for unconverted free enrollments; copy matching rows to immutable lesson IDs once when conversion pins an enrollment. Keep nullable enrollment/member associations where needed, while paid audit remains attached to the durable order.

Every paid read, completion, and signing decision must use the same entitlement contract. It combines the pinned content version, active membership, exact order, contiguous net-settled installments (or verified zero-total grant), lesson ordinal, and the existing daily timeline. The last lesson requires the full effective total. The AI completion/outbox work consumes the same required lesson IDs; it must not add a separate payment or timeline interpretation.

Media retention covers paid assets and pinned grandfather-free snapshots. The deletion barrier is keyed by Cloudflare UID, not the current video row or creator role. Deletion uncertainty never clears a reservation; retry must reconcile provider state before releasing it. Keep existing Library/free-program grants intact and reject conflicting intro/member/Task/Daily Fuel/bookmark media.

## What the current code does

- `programs` and `program_days` are currently visible under `private.can_view_program`; enrollment and day completion are separate RLS-protected tables. A member may currently enroll by inserting into `program_enrollments`, and the database checks the daily completion date. The new paid path must change these policies before any paid offer is exposed.
- `programs.platform_owned` distinguishes GYMX2 programs from coach programs. Program ownership and the coach payout destination must be snapshotted when an order is created; later profile-role or payout-account changes must not turn a coach sale into a platform sale.
- `videos.visibility` currently has only `members` and `private`. The video RLS policy grants a ready `members` video to that coach’s members and grants an assigned `private` video through `private.is_assigned_video`. The Library detail route signs a playback URL after its RLS-scoped lookup. The Program day route added in the intro foundation checks the visible day and its daily unlock before signing a creator-owned ready video.
- Armory coach payments use a destination charge, `application_fee_amount`, and `transfer_data.destination`; `platformFeeCents` rounds 10% of the item price. Platform-owned Armory items have no coach transfer. Program billing should follow those same economic rules without writing Program sales into Armory purchase rows.
- The webhook currently dispatches coach memberships, platform memberships, and Armory purchases/refunds. Program sale events need an explicit namespace branch before the generic coach invoice, Armory, and refund handlers.
- Account deletion first cancels platform membership billing, then removes owned media and deletes the auth user. Program installment schedules need an equivalent verified cancellation barrier before deletion; orders and payment audit rows must survive.

## Proposed persisted model

Additive migration: `20261118155000_program_commerce.sql`, after the intro foundation `20261118120000_program_platform_intro.sql`. It must not edit or replay the already-applied Armory or tracking migrations. This foundation migration creates only immutable content/offer versions, retained media references, the enrollment version pointer needed for a free-to-paid snapshot, and read-only eligibility helpers/policies. It does not activate checkout, webhook, schedules, promotions/redemptions, deletion tombstones, or account-deletion integration; those belong to a later billing migration after review.

1. **Foundation: `program_content_versions` and versioned lessons.** Each version has an immutable ordered lesson list with a frozen lesson count, source video ID, creator ID, Cloudflare UID, and a nullable/unknown signed-URL verification state, plus title, instructions, and other lesson fields. Lesson insertion and offer sealing lock the version row; child lessons are accepted only before the first matching offer or enrollment pin, must fit the declared count, and must follow increasing timeline-day order. Authenticated members cannot supply or change `program_enrollments.content_version_id`; they retain column-scoped insertion for ordinary free enrollment fields. Only the trusted conversion service may pin an already-offered snapshot. Existing/free media snapshots preserve their UID but must not be backfilled as `requireSignedURLs=true` without provider evidence. Only a trusted server upload-and-verify path for new Program-only media may set the signed-only flag; browser input cannot. Paid-only publication and signing fail closed unless that exact asset is verified `requireSignedURLs=true`. Paid offers and grandfathered free enrollments point at a specific revision. The foundation backfills a current revision without changing free-program behavior; the `program_enrollments` version pointer stays null for ordinary free programs until the program is converted to paid. Conversion snapshots and pins only the existing free enrollments; subsequent free-to-paid content is a separate paid revision. Editing a sold course creates a new version for future buyers; it does not change any existing order's lesson ordinals, media UID, or stage boundaries. A video-row delete or edit must not null or replace the version's provider UID. Keep the Program/version/day rows as tombstones when necessary to preserve historical mapping.
2. **Foundation: `program_offer_versions`.** Each immutable offer version records program ID, creator ID, `platform_owned`, coach Connect destination when applicable, USD list price in cents, payment choice bounds, and the content revision. Price updates publish a new offer version. Future orders retain the exact offer version. Monthly installment offers require `2 <= N <= min(12,D)` where `D` is the version's immutable lesson count; a one-lesson offer can be pay-in-full only. Never allow a future course edit to change `D` for an existing offer/order.
3. **Foundation: media retention references and guard data.** Keep an immutable reference from each lesson version to the Cloudflare UID and creator captured at snapshot time. A later paid revision may retain the same exact verified video/creator/UID tuple for the same Program, including from its immutable snapshot after the editable video row has been deleted. The UID cannot be reassigned across Programs or reused by an intro, free lesson, Task, or Daily Fuel assignment/bookmark. Revision rows keep UIDs across edits/deletes; a read-only database helper can identify retained UIDs without relying on the creator's current role. The foundation does not yet protect provider calls: Library deletion, account deletion, and community cleanup call sites must use a durable UID barrier in the later media-enforcement phase before any paid content is reachable. A database row trigger or pre-provider lookup alone does not serialize an external delete.
4. **Later billing phase: `program_promotions` and `program_promotion_reservations`.** Store codes in a server-only table, scoped to one program/offer, with starts/ends, fixed-amount or percentage discount, maximum redemptions, and per-member uniqueness. A later checkout RPC locks offer and promotion rows and creates a reservation with the order intent. A reservation is released only after the provider confirms that the exact Checkout Session is terminal and unpaid (for example, retrieved `expired`, or a reconciled `complete` Session whose current PaymentIntent is terminal and unsuccessful). A local TTL, local Stripe-create exception, network timeout, attempted cancel, or failed binding is not proof of terminality and must not release quota. Unknown provider state keeps the reservation and fences deletion until reconciled. A 100% promotion settles atomically with its no-charge grant. Promotions do not stack.
5. **Gate 1 ledger: `program_orders` and order state.** Persist nullable buyer identity without deleting order history, immutable program/content/offer versions, seller and Connect destination, USD quote, discount, fixed installment amounts and payment choice. Keep processor session IDs in service-only mutable order state. Order creation and session binding take the same buyer advisory lock as `lock_platform_membership_account_for_deletion` and reject the permanent deletion tombstone. The first verified contiguous settlement sets `access_started_on` once; a no-charge 100% promotion grant sets it atomically at creation. A delayed or abandoned Checkout intent never starts the timeline. No provider objects are created by Gate 1.
6. **Gate 1 ledger: installment events and refund markers.** `program_order_installments` stores immutable due amounts and revisioned provider status/net amounts; `program_provider_object_claims` prevents one provider object from funding multiple orders or ordinals. `program_payment_events` deduplicates provider events by exact identity, and `program_refund_markers` records refunds even if they arrive before the paid snapshot. A refund-first marker remains applied when a later payment snapshot arrives. State updates require the expected row revision; provider timestamps do not decide which snapshot wins. Promotion reservations persist until the exact bound Checkout Session is verified terminal and unpaid. The Gate 1 database contains no Checkout/webhook caller, so this contract is not active until a later reviewed integration phase.

The browser receives only a signed-in program summary, a calculated quote, and (in the later billing phase) the Checkout URL. Server-only, narrowly granted RPCs create and bind intents, reserve/settle promotion usage, record verified payments/refunds, and update entitlements. Authenticated clients cannot insert paid enrollments, completed lessons, or payment rows directly. Share one `programLessonAccess` contract across Program day reads, completion/outbox integration, and player signing: it returns only the immutable lesson IDs/ordinals whose paid-stage and normal daily gates both pass. Do not rewrite the existing free-program completion flow in the foundation phase.

### Quote and amount rules

All amounts are USD integer cents. The server computes list price, promotion, effective total, and every installment; the client never supplies a charge amount. Require every nonzero Stripe charge—including a pay-in-full total—to meet the applicable Stripe minimum (currently $0.50 USD for the ordinary USD charge case). For `N` installments, allocate `floor(effectiveTotal / N)` cents to each of the first `N-1` payments and put the remainder in the last payment; reject that choice if any part falls below the minimum. A zero effective total creates an auditable fully funded free order and enrollment without a Stripe object or charge. A one-lesson course (`D=1`) cannot use installments; a 100%-discount grant is still allowed because it creates no payment.

For an installment order with `D` immutable lesson ordinals and `k` consecutive, fully cleared installments out of `N`, the paid lesson ceiling is `floor(D * k / N)`, where `2 <= N <= min(12,D)`. The 12-payment authoring cap keeps the plan finite while still requiring each installment to open at least one lesson; the final lesson never appears before the last payment. Lesson `i` is accessible only if `i <= paidCeiling` and its ordinary daily timeline is also open. Pay-in-full has `N=1` and opens every ordinal only after the full effective total settles, so it lifts the payment ceiling but not the daily timeline. For partial refunds, count only consecutive installments whose net settled cents still cover their full immutable due amount; never skip a refunded unpaid stage to open a later one. Free orders retain the existing daily rule. Persist `D`, ordinals, and content revision in the order so later edits cannot shift the cap.

**Free-to-paid boundary:** free programs stay dynamic under the current behavior until conversion. At conversion, snapshot the existing curriculum as the last free revision and bind current free enrollments to it. Those people keep their existing free enrollment/access and are not retroactively charged. The paid offer points at a new content revision. Any new lessons added after conversion belong only to that paid revision and are not appended to old free enrollments. New enrollees use the current paid offer. Existing free-program RLS, completion history, and daily timeline remain unchanged for grandfathered enrollments.

## Stripe payment flow

The installed SDK is Stripe `19.3.0`. Its type definitions support `Checkout.SessionCreateParams.PaymentIntentData.setup_future_usage`, `SubscriptionScheduleCreateParams.start_date`, finite phase `duration` (`month` and `interval_count`), phase `price_data` (including a monthly recurring amount), phase/schedule `application_fee_percent` and `transfer_data`, and `end_behavior: "cancel"`. Use `duration`; the installed API type does not model a legacy `iterations` field. A Schedule phase `price_data` requires an existing Stripe Product ID, so the implementation needs one stable server-managed Product per paid Program (or another verified Product lifecycle) before it can build installment Price data.

### Pay in full

Create a one-time Checkout Session with the server-calculated effective total. On a signed webhook, retrieve and verify the current Session and PaymentIntent against the exact order, buyer, currency, amount, sale namespace, and seller split, then record the settled payment idempotently. Only settlement unlocks the full payment-stage ceiling.

### Monthly installments

1. Display the first charge, number and cadence of future charges, any different final remainder, total, saved-payment-method use, and cancellation behavior. Record affirmative agreement to those specific future charges and the terms version. Stripe’s `off_session` saved-method notice is supplementary; Stripe’s compliance guidance says the merchant must collect and retain consent describing the transactions, timing/frequency, amount calculation, and cancellation policy.
2. Create the first Checkout Session in `payment` mode for installment 1. Set `payment_intent_data.setup_future_usage="off_session"` and ensure a Stripe Customer is created or supplied, so the payment method is attached for reuse. Restrict installment checkout to card initially; do not accept asynchronous methods unless their later off-session and settlement behavior has a separate verified path.
3. Persist the first settlement timestamp and a fixed calendar anchor (original billing day and end-of-month behavior) in the order. After the first payment is verified, grant only its stage and create one finite Subscription Schedule with a stable idempotency key tied to the order. Set its start to the next monthly anchor, not `now`, so a delayed webhook cannot cause an immediate catch-up charge. It contains only the remaining `N-1` scheduled charges, uses `end_behavior="cancel"`, and uses the saved customer/payment method. Use at most two monthly phases: an equal-amount phase lasting `N-2` months, then the one-month final remainder phase. If there is no separate remainder, one phase is sufficient. Set phase transition prorations to `none` so a fixed-total plan cannot generate an adjustment charge. If setup arrives after the next anchor is past, keep it pending and require an explicit buyer resume/updated-date confirmation; never backdate or bill immediately without that action.
4. For coach-owned Programs, the initial PaymentIntent uses the existing destination account and a 10% application fee on that installment. Each Schedule phase uses `application_fee_percent=10` and the same immutable `transfer_data.destination`. GYMX2-owned Programs have no destination transfer or coach application fee. Do not infer ownership from the buyer’s current profile role. If the connected destination is unavailable, fail closed before checkout; if it becomes unavailable later, do not unlock an unpaid installment.
5. Persist a durable `schedule_setup_pending` state before attempting schedule creation. Schedule creation failure must not remove the first settled stage or authorize an unbounded subscription; return a retryable webhook failure and reconcile with the same idempotency key. If the provider response is ambiguous, retrieve/reconcile the exact schedule by its idempotent order key and metadata; do not assume failure or create a second schedule. Until the finite schedule is verified, show “first installment paid; remaining payments are being set up” and do not say the plan is complete. No verified schedule means no future charge.

**Fixed-total invoice isolation:** Do not attach a Program installment Schedule to a Stripe Customer shared with coach memberships, platform memberships, or unrelated purchases. Create an isolated customer for the Program order and keep it free of unrelated pending invoice items, customer-balance credits/debits, coupons, tax settings, and automatic invoice adjustments. Each invoice must be verified against the immutable order before it can be collected: exactly the expected Program installment line(s), currency, amount, and Connect split. If an invoice contains an unexpected item, tax, balance adjustment, discount, or amount, stop collection and reconcile it without charging the buyer. Do not count unrelated account credits toward the fixed Program total. Sales tax remains disabled for this installment flow until the quote and consent explicitly account for it. A shared-customer fallback is not supported unless it can prove equivalent isolation for every invoice and namespace.

The amount schedule is a fixed sum: the first Checkout payment plus the `N-1` scheduled invoice amounts equals the effective quote exactly. `end_behavior="cancel"` is required because Stripe’s default is `release`, which leaves the underlying subscription running after a Schedule ends. Stripe documents that `cancel` cancels the underlying subscription when its Schedule ends. The final implementation must verify actual Schedule retrieval against the stored phase count, prices, destination, application fee, customer, and cancellation behavior before reporting setup complete.

Do not offer early payoff in the first release. Cancellation serializes under the order lock, records `cancel_requested` before provider calls, and blocks new checkout/schedule creation. Expire and verify any exact open Checkout Session. Cancel a Schedule and its underlying subscription with `invoice_now=false` and `prorate=false`, then retrieve/reconcile every in-flight PaymentIntent and invoice. A successful payment already in flight is recorded and may support its stage; a failed or unresolved payment grants no stage. Do not clear the deletion/cancellation tombstone while any provider operation is ambiguous. Only after Stripe confirms no future schedule/collection remains may account deletion continue. If the provider is unavailable or create/bind status is unknown, deletion stays pending and the buyer retains the account until reconciliation. A future payoff flow must use the same lock and perform this cancellation/reconciliation before charging the remainder.

### Webhook and refunds

Use a namespace such as `kind="program_sale"` and a strict `program_order_id`; put the same order identity in Schedule/phase metadata so subscription invoice metadata can be correlated. Dispatch Program events before coach membership and Armory handlers. Handle the verified initial Checkout payment, paid subscription invoices, failed/action-required invoices, schedule completion/abort/cancellation, refunds, and disputes. Retrieve current Stripe objects and bind every event to the stored customer, order, expected amount, installment, currency, and immutable destination. Event IDs provide replay deduplication; a database revision/CAS protects concurrent reads. A signed event with the wrong namespace or mismatched object is acknowledged or rejected without entering another billing handler.

Grant an installment only after its invoice/payment is currently paid. Failure or a required customer action adds no stage. Early cancellation stops all future installments and retains only the stages supported by payments already settled. Full or partial refunds update net-settled amounts; a refunded installment no longer clears its stage if the net amount is below its scheduled amount. Recompute the stage ceiling from contiguous cleared installments, but never delete completion history. A paid-in-full order loses paid-only access if its net payment falls below the effective total. A refund-first marker prevents a later replay of the original paid event from restoring access. Preserve the separate behavior of existing Armory and coach refunds.

On buyer account deletion, mark deletion pending, cancel and verify every nonterminal Program Schedule, then complete deletion while preserving order/payment audit and existing paid-stage history; set the buyer profile reference to null and remove personal contact data according to the deletion policy. If Stripe cannot confirm cancellation, keep the account and retry rather than deleting it while recurring charges may remain. Creator deletion with sales should be blocked or handled as an audited support transfer; do not cascade-delete paid orders or the seller snapshot. Deleting a Program with sales should archive it or use the same audited support path, never cascade-delete its paid order ledger.

## Library and entitlement boundary

The existing Library intentionally serves coach-member videos and Task-assigned private videos. Daily Fuel/Task or signed-in Library access can also be a preexisting grant. A page-level lock does not protect those alternate paths. Add a distinct `program_only` visibility mode for lesson media that is explicitly sold with a paid Program. It is absent from the normal Library, Task/Daily Fuel selectors, and public UID projections, and readable only through a single paid-stage predicate used by:

- `program_days` full-row RLS and day-completion inserts;
- the Program day playback signer;
- `videos` RLS, the Library detail route, and the thumbnail/token routes;
- any current or future API that returns the video UID or signs Cloudflare playback.

For `program_only`, neither coach-member membership nor Task/Daily Fuel assignment is a grant. The predicate checks the exact program revision/day association, authenticated buyer order, current net-paid lesson ordinal, and normal daily timeline. The Program-only branch cannot fall through to `private.is_assigned_video`. Admin/creator preview remains a separate management path. New protected uploads must be created with Cloudflare `requireSignedURLs=true`; an app-signed iframe URL does not protect an asset whose provider UID is public. Fail closed if Cloudflare does not confirm the asset is signed-only. Store that verified provider setting and immutable UID in the paid version. Cloudflare tokens are signed and limited-lived; the current `getPlayerUrl` helper documents a one-hour lifetime, so cancellation/refund stops future token issuance but cannot invalidate a token already issued.

Keep `members` video behavior unchanged. A video already visible to coach members, any free-program enrollment, or a Task/Daily Fuel assignment cannot become paid-only silently. Reject using it as protected paid content and tell the creator to upload a new Program-only asset; do not flip visibility and revoke an unrelated existing grant. Likewise reject conversion of a video that has live Task/Daily Fuel assignments. Existing private assigned videos continue to use those grants and must not be presented as paid-only Program lessons. A new paid-only asset is never added to Task/Daily Fuel. The same unrestricted media may continue through its current free Program path, but it cannot serve as exclusive paid media.

The provider-deletion guard is checked before every Cloudflare delete request, including Library deletion, creator account deletion, and community cleanup; database triggers alone are too late because provider deletion precedes row deletion. It uses immutable retained UIDs and version/order/reservation references, not current profile ownership or role. Edit/replacement keeps all retained UIDs available to old versions. A failed guard read denies provider deletion.

Keep listing and access distinct: signed-in members who meet paid-Program eligibility may see an allowlisted Program offer card/details without receiving full `program_days` data. A safe summary projection exposes only sellable metadata. Full day instructions, video IDs, completion inserts, and signed media require active Regular/Premium eligibility; coach-owned Programs also require published status and the buyer's coach link, plus the paid lesson ordinal. Billing, receipts, and cancellation remain reachable after plan expiration. Keep the current free Program path and its membership rules intact.

- With `N<=min(12,D)`, the first `N-1` installment states never expose ordinal `D`; the final lesson becomes available only when net payments reach the full effective total. `D=1` permits pay-in-full only. A zero-effective-total promo or explicit free price uses a full grant without checkout, invoice, or Stripe call.
- Turning a free course paid preserves every prior enrollment on its free snapshot and does not charge it. Lessons added in the new paid revision remain invisible to prior free enrollments. New buyers use the current offer/content revision.
- Editing/deleting the current video row, changing creator role, leaving a coach, or deleting an account cannot delete the Cloudflare asset while a retained version/free-enrollment snapshot/published offer/nonterminal checkout/paid order references its UID. The provider guard runs before external deletion; uncertainty denies deletion.
- Price/content revision after sale leaves the existing buyer’s quote, schedule, stage-day denominator, lesson version, Connect destination, and completed history unchanged.
- Concurrent redemption at a promotion cap cannot exceed quota; one member cannot redeem twice. Local TTL, local create exception, failed bind, and timeout do not release a reservation. Only an exact verified provider-terminal Session permits release; 100% redemption makes one fully funded, no-Stripe grant.
- Refund-before-paid insert and duplicate/out-of-order payment/refund events do not restore net-paid balance or reopen a stage. Partial refunds reduce net cleared stages; completion rows remain historical.
- Profile deletion tombstones and fences new checkout/binds/schedule creation; open Sessions are verified expired or reconciled, and ambiguous Session/Schedule/PaymentIntent creation blocks deletion. Late webhook payment/refund is recorded but never recreates deleted access. Schedule cancellation uses `invoice_now=false`, `prorate=false`, reconciles in-flight invoices/PaymentIntents, and prevents unresolved future collections after deletion. Creator deletion cannot cascade orders or delete retained media.
- The monthly anchor is stored from the first settled payment. Delayed schedule setup never starts `now` to backcharge; it remains pending until the buyer explicitly resumes with a disclosed future anchor.

## Confirmed foundation decisions

- Installments require `2 <= N <= min(12,D)`; pay-in-full is the only choice for a one-lesson Program. Gate 1 does not yet reopen an order after a terminal Session or full refund; the buyer/program order fence stays closed until the billing phase implements provider-verified restart semantics. The lesson ceiling is `floor(D*k/N)` and the final ordinal stays closed until the full effective total is net settled.
- Content revisions and offer versions are immutable. The foundation backfills a current snapshot and leaves existing free enrollments on their current dynamic path. At conversion, existing enrollments are pinned to a free snapshot and are not charged.
- Paid lessons use immutable media UID references. Existing/free videos are not marked signed-only without provider evidence. A later media-enforcement phase must guard every provider-delete call site and verify protected uploads before paid content is reachable.
- Program purchases require a signed-in member with active Regular or Premium access. Coach-owned Programs additionally require a published Program and a coach link. The existing free-program rules remain unchanged.
- Checkout, schedules, promotion reservations/redemptions, deletion tombstones, webhook handling, and call-site media enforcement are later phases. The foundation must not be released by itself as paid access.
- The active-membership predicate is only a buyer-eligibility prerequisite, not purchase entitlement. Later reads and media signing must also prove the immutable order, settled payment state, and lesson stage; ordinary free access remains on its existing path.
- Installment cancellation will use `invoice_now=false` and `prorate=false`, reconcile in-flight invoices and PaymentIntents, and block account deletion while provider state is ambiguous.

Before billing is enabled, the product owner still needs to settle customer-facing cancellation/refund wording and the retry window for a failed installment. Sales tax is outside the initial fixed-total installment flow; enabling it requires a new quote, consent, and invoice-isolation design. Connect fees must be calculated only on the agreed Program charge base if taxes or other invoice items are later introduced.
