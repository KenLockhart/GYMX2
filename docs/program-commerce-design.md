## Scope

Programs will be purchasable only by signed-in accounts in the Programs area. Guest checkout remains Armory-only. A member buyer needs active Regular/Premium eligibility for both coach and platform-owned paid Programs; a coach-owned Program additionally requires that it is published and the buyer is linked to that coach. Enforce these requirements in the signed-in UI, paid RPCs, day access, and media signing, not only through layout auth. Own billing/receipt/cancellation routes remain available after membership expires. Preserve current free-program eligibility and daily-unlock behavior rather than globally broadening or restricting its RLS. Telehealth and medical-product purchasing are out of scope.

The buyer chooses one of two fixed-total payment choices:

- **Pay in full:** one charge for the effective total.
- **Monthly installments:** a finite number of charges that sum to the effective total. This is not an indefinitely renewing subscription.

Payment stages open only when the corresponding payment has settled. A lesson can never be exposed before its immutable ordinal stage is paid, and the final lesson stays locked until the effective discounted total is fully settled. Full payment or a fully funded 100% promotion removes the payment-stage cap, but the existing daily timeline still applies.

### Completion and deletion invariants

`program_day_completions` points to mutable day rows with cascading deletion, so it cannot remain the history store for pinned or purchased revisions. The versioned completion table must reference immutable lesson/version IDs and survive changes to `program_days` or deletion of the buyer account. Preserve the old completion path for unconverted free enrollments; copy matching rows to immutable lesson IDs once when conversion pins an enrollment. Keep nullable enrollment/member associations where needed, while paid audit remains attached to the durable order.

Every paid read, completion, and signing decision must use the same entitlement contract. It combines the pinned content version, active membership, exact order, contiguous net-settled installments (or verified zero-total grant), lesson ordinal, and the existing daily timeline. The last lesson requires the full effective total. The AI completion/outbox work consumes the same required lesson IDs; it must not add a separate payment or timeline interpretation.

Media retention covers paid assets and pinned grandfather-free snapshots. The deletion barrier is keyed by Cloudflare UID, not the current video row or creator role. Deletion uncertainty never clears a reservation; retry must reconcile provider state before releasing it. Keep existing Library/free-program grants intact and reject conflicting intro/member/Task/Daily Fuel/bookmark media.

## Confirmed payment policies

Retry a failed installment for seven days using the same exact standalone Invoice and PaymentIntent; unpaid stages stay locked. Cancellation stops future charges while preserving stages already paid. Refund requests are reviewed under the existing refund policy; do not automate or promise a refund. Sales tax remains outside the initial fixed-total flow. Each Program order uses an isolated per-order Customer and exact-amount invoice verification; it must never collect unrelated balances or pending invoice items.

## Proposed persisted model

Additive migration: `20261118155000_program_commerce.sql`, after the intro foundation `20261118120000_program_platform_intro.sql`. It must not edit or replay the already-applied Armory or tracking migrations. This foundation migration creates only immutable content/offer versions, retained media references, the enrollment version pointer needed for a free-to-paid snapshot, and read-only eligibility helpers/policies. It does not activate checkout, webhook, finite installment collection, promotions/redemptions, deletion tombstones, or account-deletion integration; those belong to later reviewed gates.

1. **Foundation: `program_content_versions` and versioned lessons.** Each version has an immutable ordered lesson list with a frozen lesson count, source video ID, creator ID, Cloudflare UID, and a nullable/unknown signed-URL verification state, plus title, instructions, and other lesson fields. Lesson insertion and offer sealing lock the version row; child lessons are accepted only before the first matching offer or enrollment pin, must fit the declared count, and must follow increasing timeline-day order. Authenticated members cannot supply or change `program_enrollments.content_version_id`; they retain column-scoped insertion for ordinary free enrollment fields. Only the trusted conversion service may pin an already-offered snapshot. Existing/free media snapshots preserve their UID but must not be backfilled as `requireSignedURLs=true` without provider evidence. Only a trusted server upload-and-verify path for new Program-only media may set the signed-only flag; browser input cannot. Paid-only publication and signing fail closed unless that exact asset is verified `requireSignedURLs=true`. Paid offers and grandfathered free enrollments point at a specific revision. The foundation backfills a current revision without changing free-program behavior; the `program_enrollments` version pointer stays null for ordinary free programs until the program is converted to paid. Conversion snapshots and pins only the existing free enrollments; subsequent free-to-paid content is a separate paid revision. Editing a sold course creates a new version for future buyers; it does not change any existing order's lesson ordinals, media UID, or stage boundaries. A video-row delete or edit must not null or replace the version's provider UID. Keep the Program/version/day rows as tombstones when necessary to preserve historical mapping.
2. **Foundation: `program_offer_versions`.** Each immutable offer version records program ID, creator ID, `platform_owned`, coach Connect destination when applicable, USD list price in cents, payment choice bounds, and the content revision. Price updates publish a new offer version. Future orders retain the exact offer version. Monthly installment offers require `2 <= N <= min(12,D)` where `D` is the version's immutable lesson count; a one-lesson offer can be pay-in-full only. Never allow a future course edit to change `D` for an existing offer/order.
3. **Foundation: media retention references and guard data.** Keep an immutable reference from each lesson version to the Cloudflare UID and creator captured at snapshot time. A later paid revision may retain the same exact verified video/creator/UID tuple for the same Program, including from its immutable snapshot after the editable video row has been deleted. The UID cannot be reassigned across Programs or reused by an intro, free lesson, Task, or Daily Fuel assignment/bookmark. Revision rows keep UIDs across edits/deletes; a read-only database helper can identify retained UIDs without relying on the creator's current role. The foundation does not yet protect provider calls: Library deletion, account deletion, and community cleanup call sites must use a durable UID barrier in the later media-enforcement phase before any paid content is reachable. A database row trigger or pre-provider lookup alone does not serialize an external delete.
4. **Later billing phase: `program_promotions` and `program_promotion_reservations`.** Store codes in a server-only table, scoped to one program/offer, with starts/ends, fixed-amount or percentage discount, maximum redemptions, and per-member uniqueness. A later checkout RPC locks offer and promotion rows and creates a reservation with the order intent. A reservation is released only after the provider confirms that the exact Checkout Session is terminal and unpaid (for example, retrieved `expired`, or a reconciled `complete` Session whose current PaymentIntent is terminal and unsuccessful). A local TTL, local Stripe-create exception, network timeout, attempted cancel, or failed binding is not proof of terminality and must not release quota. Unknown provider state keeps the reservation and fences deletion until reconciled. A 100% promotion settles atomically with its no-charge grant. Promotions do not stack.
5. **Gate 1 ledger: `program_orders` and order state.** Persist nullable buyer identity without deleting order history, immutable program/content/offer versions, seller and Connect destination, USD quote, discount, fixed installment amounts and payment choice. Keep processor session IDs in service-only mutable order state. Order creation and session binding take the same buyer advisory lock as `lock_platform_membership_account_for_deletion` and reject the permanent deletion tombstone. The first verified contiguous settlement sets `access_started_on` once; a no-charge 100% promotion grant sets it atomically at creation. A delayed or abandoned Checkout intent never starts the timeline. No provider objects are created by Gate 1.
6. **Gate 1 ledger: installment events and refund markers.** `program_order_installments` stores immutable due amounts and revisioned provider status/net amounts; `program_provider_object_claims` prevents one provider object from funding multiple orders or ordinals. `program_payment_events` deduplicates provider events by exact identity, and `program_refund_markers` records refunds even if they arrive before the paid snapshot. A refund-first marker remains applied when a later payment snapshot arrives. State updates require the expected row revision; provider timestamps do not decide which snapshot wins. Promotion reservations persist until the exact bound Checkout Session is verified terminal and unpaid. The Gate 1 database contains no Checkout/webhook caller, so this contract is not active until a later reviewed integration phase.

The browser receives only a signed-in program summary, a calculated quote, and (after server contracts and release gates pass) the Checkout URL. Server-only, narrowly granted RPCs create and bind intents, reserve/settle promotion usage, record verified payments/refunds, and update entitlements. Authenticated clients cannot insert paid enrollments, completed lessons, or payment rows directly. Share one `programLessonAccess` contract across Program day reads, completion/outbox integration, and player signing: it returns only the immutable lesson IDs/ordinals whose paid-stage and normal daily gates both pass. Do not rewrite the existing free-program completion flow in the foundation phase.

### Quote and amount rules

All amounts are USD integer cents. The server computes list price, promotion, effective total, and every installment; the client never supplies a charge amount. Require every nonzero Stripe charge—including a pay-in-full total—to meet the applicable Stripe minimum (currently $0.50 USD for the ordinary USD charge case). For `N` installments, allocate `floor(effectiveTotal / N)` cents to each of the first `N-1` payments and put the remainder in the last payment; reject that choice if any part falls below the minimum. A zero effective total creates an auditable fully funded free order and enrollment without a Stripe object or charge. A one-lesson course (`D=1`) cannot use installments; a 100%-discount grant is still allowed because it creates no payment.

For an installment order with `D` immutable lesson ordinals and `k` consecutive, fully cleared installments out of `N`, the paid lesson ceiling is `floor(D * k / N)`, where `2 <= N <= min(12,D)`. The 12-payment authoring cap keeps the plan finite while still requiring each installment to open at least one lesson; the final lesson never appears before the last payment. Lesson `i` is accessible only if `i <= paidCeiling` and its ordinary daily timeline is also open. Pay-in-full has `N=1` and opens every ordinal only after the full effective total settles, so it lifts the payment ceiling but not the daily timeline. For partial refunds, count only consecutive installments whose net settled cents still cover their full immutable due amount; never skip a refunded unpaid stage to open a later one. Free orders retain the existing daily rule. Persist `D`, ordinals, and content revision in the order so later edits cannot shift the cap.

**Free-to-paid boundary:** free programs stay dynamic under the current behavior until conversion. At conversion, snapshot the existing curriculum as the last free revision and bind current free enrollments to it. Those people keep their existing free enrollment/access and are not retroactively charged. The paid offer points at a new content revision. Any new lessons added after conversion belong only to that paid revision and are not appended to old free enrollments. New enrollees use the current paid offer. Existing free-program RLS, completion history, and daily timeline remain unchanged for grandfathered enrollments.

## Stripe payment flow

The installed SDK is Stripe `19.3.0`. Gate 4 deliberately uses a finite application timetable and controlled standalone Invoices. Stripe Subscription and Subscription Schedule objects are not created. This preserves the fixed-total monthly terms while keeping each future collection under the same exact-invoice inspection and explicit-pay path.

### First charge and immutable request

The buyer reviews the fixed total, payment count, disclosed monthly dates, final remainder if any, saved-card/off-session consent, seven-day retry period, and cancellation rule before accepting. Consent is tied to the exact quote digest and dates. Initial payment uses one card-only Checkout Session in `payment` mode. Installment plans set `payment_intent_data.setup_future_usage="off_session"`; pay-in-full does not request future-use consent. Coach sales snapshot their Connect destination and 10% fee; platform-owned sales have no destination or coach fee.

Before the first provider create, the adapter uses the same captured unscoped Stripe client to retrieve the platform Account ID and Balance `livemode`. The database stores that merchant scope with the durable Customer intent before sending Customer creation; the scope is immutable. A failed scope read blocks every create. Later provider objects must match the captured mode and carry the expected account/mode metadata where the application created it. Mode is never inferred from an API key prefix.

Before provider creates, the database stores the isolated per-order Customer intent and immutable Checkout attempt, including request digest, stable idempotency key, first-request timestamp, and the exact success/cancel URLs. An uncertain create response is reconciled from complete provider inventory. If no matching object exists, the identical body may be retried with the original key for at most 23 hours from that first timestamp; the key and timestamp are never reset. After that safe provider-idempotency window, or after cancellation/deletion fencing, absence from a search is not treated as terminal proof. The order stays pending for manual reconciliation.

An ordinary expired unpaid Checkout retry requires an explicit buyer request and complete current inventory proving the old Session is expired/unpaid, its exact PaymentIntent is canceled with zero received, and the isolated Customer has no unrelated invoices, pending items, Subscription, or Schedule. Each retry is a new append-only attempt under the same one-order fence and immutable quote. Failed, processing, or canceled attempt facts do not claim the canonical Gate 1 PI for that installment. Only a verified current settled PI reaches the canonical ledger. If an earlier attempt later reports success after terminal retry, record the actual event and object as a financial conflict for manual review; do not silently discard payment, issue a replacement charge, or fabricate a refund.

### App-managed monthly collection

The accepted consent stores date-only monthly anchors in the `America/New_York` calendar, matching the database's due-date gate. The first successful charge fixes the day-of-month and end-of-month rule from the first disclosed future date, leaving only `N-1` future dates. UI must render those exact dates with that timezone context; it must not convert or rewrite the stored date keys. A durable scheduled job invokes the installment helper only for the exact next disclosed anchor. If service misses an anchor before any pay attempt, require the buyer to confirm a new future date; never create stacked catch-up charges or backdated immediate collection. Each installment amount stays immutable and the amounts including the first Checkout charge sum exactly to the stored effective total.

For one future charge, the helper:

1. Rechecks buyer/program eligibility, order/cancellation/deletion fences, prior contiguous settlement, current invoice revision, saved-card consent, retry deadline, and full Customer inventory.
2. Creates one draft invoice for the isolated Customer with `collection_method="charge_automatically"`, `auto_advance=false`, `pending_invoice_items_behavior="exclude"`, no discounts or tax, and the saved card as default. The 10% application fee and immutable destination are set on the invoice for coach-owned programs.
3. Creates exactly one invoice item with the exact `invoice` ID, amount, currency and installment metadata. It lists every invoice line and verifies that the exact item is the only line before binding it.
4. Finalizes that exact invoice with `auto_advance=false`, then retrieves the current Invoice, all lines and its exact default InvoicePayment-to-PaymentIntent association. The installed SDK exposes that association on InvoicePayment; do not infer it from a legacy Invoice field or from PaymentIntent metadata. Future-invoice PaymentIntents may have empty metadata; their ownership comes from the verified Invoice, exact InvoicePayment association, Customer, amount/currency, saved card, provider scope, and Connect split. Before any pay call it checks invoice ID, Customer, status, total, starting/ending balance, discounts, tax, shipping, amount paid/remaining, exact InvoicePayment count/type/default flag, line ID/amount/currency/tax/discount metadata, PI amount/status, saved card, Connect destination, and application fee.
5. Persists the first collection-start timestamp and an exact `+7 days` retry deadline with the durable pay-attempt intent before explicitly paying the exact Invoice with that saved card and `off_session=true`. The start time is set once on the first pay intent and never resets on retries, restart or duplicate events. The deadline is inclusive at the exact timestamp and closed one millisecond later. A successful provider response returns “awaiting ledger”; only the separate verified-settlement wrapper records the actual Stripe event and invokes Gate 1. No helper directly grants a stage.

All invoice, line-item, finalize and pay attempts are durable and use stable operation keys. Unknown create responses reconcile the exact Invoice or item from complete current inventory; when there is no object, the same immutable create request/key may be retried only within the same 23-hour safety window. A failed, unknown or action-required pay remains bound to its same Invoice/PI; no new Invoice or hidden PI is created. The seven-day retry clock begins at the first explicit pay intent, not invoice setup. It does not extend provider idempotency. Once the Invoice is known, payment retries use only its same PI and exact saved method. A draft invoice left uncollected past its disclosed date requires buyer date confirmation before collection; this first release has no date-change surface, so that case remains pending for manual reconciliation.

Stripe documents that `auto_advance=false` disables automatic invoice finalization/collection, invoice emails, reminders, and retry attempts. Email receipts remain configurable in Stripe settings, so receipt behavior must be intentionally disclosed and configured. The application does not call `sendInvoice`; the controlled standalone Invoice is not emailed to solicit payment. Tax remains disabled for this fixed-total flow. Unexpected credit/balance/tax/discount/line or seller-split evidence quarantines collection before explicit pay.

### Cancellation, account deletion and reconciliation

Cancellation serializes under the buyer/order fence and records its durable intent before any provider mutation. It stops future app-managed jobs, expires only the exact current open Checkout Session after verifying ownership, voids exact unpaid open future invoices, and deletes only exact owned draft invoices after a durable pre-delete snapshot and complete inventory checks. Unknown finalization/void outcomes stay pending until exact drain evidence resolves them; a finalized invoice cannot use the draft-delete path. It never calls `Invoice.pay` for future stages. Already-settled stages remain. Refunds remain subject to the existing review policy; no refund is initiated automatically.

Draft Invoice deletion stores its exact pre-delete snapshot, captured account/mode, provider idempotency key and proof state before sending DELETE. A later worker reads that proof through the service-only `get_program_billing_draft_invoice_delete` RPC and retrieves the same Invoice in the captured scope. A qualified `resource_missing` 404 reconciles only when the exact Invoice is absent from the complete Customer, Invoice, pending-item, PaymentIntent, Subscription and Schedule inventories; a bare 404 or empty search cannot establish ownership or deletion. If the Invoice is still present, the worker may continue the same unknown cleanup intent only after proving the exact draft and lines are unchanged, with no InvoicePayments or new PaymentIntents, and all inventories complete. It reuses the original proof and idempotency key; changed, finalized, paid or ambiguous resources stay blocked or pending. Account-deletion promotion keeps only exact, persisted cleanup intents for Checkout expiry, Invoice void and draft Invoice deletion alive; money and create intents are terminated under the existing fence.

If buyer cancellation or deletion commits while a provider create/finalize/pay call is already unknown, a drain-only service RPC may reconcile only the exact existing object from that persisted operation, original request digest, immutable order/attempt metadata, Customer, and stored merchant scope. It acquires the account mutex before the buyer mutex and never retries a possibly creating call, finalizes, pays, refunds, settles Gate 1, or grants access. An already-paid contradiction is durably recorded for manual review; an unlocatable or incomplete object remains pending. Exact inventory then proves provider-side expiry/void before cancellation completes.

Buyer account deletion uses the frozen media lifecycle coordinator plus a registered `program_billing` cleanup component. The component must prove the exact Customer, every Session/PI/Invoice/item, no subscriptions or schedules, and no pending collection before recording its full-resource proof. Unknown provider state blocks deletion until the exact drain path resolves it; a late paid/refund/terminal contradiction is durably audited with the real event ID and current provider evidence, but cannot create access after the permanent account fence. Shared webhook dispatch and deletion-call-site integration are separate work and remain release blockers.

The Stripe adapter converts raw Customer fields (`discount`, `tax_exempt`, and `invoice_credit_balance`) to the normalized `ProgramStripeCustomer` DTO fields `discountId`, `taxExempt`, and `invoiceCreditBalance`. Billing RPC snapshots and cleanup inventories use those camel-case DTO keys consistently. Raw Stripe wire objects used by direct API evidence retain their provider field names; code must not mix those raw keys with normalized DTO keys in one proof object.

New protected uploads must be created with Cloudflare `requireSignedURLs=true`; an app-signed iframe URL does not protect an asset whose provider UID is public. Fail closed if Cloudflare does not confirm the asset is signed-only. Store that verified provider setting and immutable UID in the paid version. Cloudflare tokens are signed and limited-lived; the current `getPlayerUrl` helper documents a one-hour lifetime, so cancellation/refund stops future token issuance but cannot invalidate a token already issued.

Keep `members` video behavior unchanged. A video already visible to coach members, any free-program enrollment, or a Task/Daily Fuel assignment cannot become paid-only silently. Reject using it as protected paid content and tell the creator to upload a new Program-only asset; do not flip visibility and revoke an unrelated existing grant. Likewise reject conversion of a video that has live Task/Daily Fuel assignments. Existing private assigned videos continue to use those grants and must not be presented as paid-only Program lessons. A new paid-only asset is never added to Task/Daily Fuel. The same unrestricted media may continue through its current free Program path, but it cannot serve as exclusive paid media.

The provider-deletion guard is checked before every Cloudflare delete request, including Library deletion, creator account deletion, and community cleanup; database triggers alone are too late because provider deletion precedes row deletion. It uses immutable retained UIDs and version/order/reservation references, not current profile ownership or role. Edit/replacement keeps all retained UIDs available to old versions. A failed guard read denies provider deletion.

Keep listing and access distinct: signed-in members who meet paid-Program eligibility may see an allowlisted Program offer card/details without receiving full `program_days` data. A safe summary projection exposes only sellable metadata. Full day instructions, video IDs, completion inserts, and signed media require active Regular/Premium eligibility; coach-owned Programs also require published status and the buyer's coach link, plus the paid lesson ordinal. Billing, receipts, and cancellation remain reachable after plan expiration. Keep the current free Program path and its membership rules intact.

## Confirmed foundation decisions

- Installments require `2 <= N <= min(12,D)`; pay-in-full is the only choice for a one-lesson Program. Gate 1's one-order fence remains unique; Gate 4 may append a same-order Checkout attempt only after an explicit buyer request and terminal Session/unpaid PI inventory proof. Changed-quote/released-promotion repurchase remains an initial release boundary. The lesson ceiling is `floor(D*k/N)` and the final ordinal stays closed until the full effective total is net settled.
- Content revisions and offer versions are immutable. The foundation backfills a current snapshot and leaves existing free enrollments on their current dynamic path. At conversion, existing enrollments are pinned to a free snapshot and are not charged.
- Paid lessons use immutable media UID references. Existing/free videos are not marked signed-only without provider evidence. A later media-enforcement phase must guard every provider-delete call site and verify protected uploads before paid content is reachable.
- Program purchases require a signed-in member with active Regular or Premium access. Coach-owned Programs additionally require a published Program and a coach link. The existing free-program rules remain unchanged.
- Gate 4's local database/helper packet is not a release by itself. Shared webhook dispatch, account-deletion caller, durable job/cron, Program buyer UI, and call-site media enforcement still require separate integration and review.
- The active-membership predicate is only a buyer-eligibility prerequisite, not purchase entitlement. Later reads and media signing must also prove the immutable order, settled payment state, and lesson stage; ordinary free access remains on its existing path.
- Installment cancellation stops the app-managed invoice job, reconciles exact current Invoices and PaymentIntents, and blocks account deletion while provider state is ambiguous.

Sales tax is outside the initial fixed-total installment flow; enabling it requires a new quote, consent, and invoice-isolation design. Connect fees must be calculated only on the agreed Program charge base if taxes or other invoice items are later introduced.

## Program authoring backend (Gate 6)

The bounded authoring backend keeps editable lesson candidates in private draft tables and publishes content, offer, and promotion terms through narrow service-only RPCs. Candidate media is only a UUID selection; publication re-reads the creator, ready video, signed-media evidence, source binding, cleanup state, and cross-surface exclusivity. The authoring adapter derives the actor from the signed-in coach/admin profile, validates strict DTOs, and never accepts seller, Connect account, UID, fee, or media-readiness facts from the caller. Paid mutations remain disabled unless `PROGRAM_PAID_AUTHORING_ENABLED=true` is set server-side.

First paid publication uses the existing 200 free-snapshot and fence-release sequence in one transaction. Existing free enrollments retain their pinned snapshot and completion history. A new Program with no legacy days may publish text-only paid lessons with an empty free snapshot; a selected video that disappears remains visible in the draft and blocks publication until the author explicitly clears or replaces it with a successful draft revision. Full content publication sets the parent Program published; price-only and promotion changes preserve its current published state. Same-Program paid media may be reused only after fresh source and exclusivity checks. Paid-to-free conversion remains unsupported in this release.
