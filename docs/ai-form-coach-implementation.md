## Design decisions and safety contracts

1. **Use the repository architecture.** The repository has no Supabase Edge Functions. The prompt's pricing-save, checkout, and summary functions will use Next Server Actions/Route Handlers and Postgres RPCs; scheduled retry/expiry/retention work will use the established Vercel cron route. This is the codebase convention and keeps payments/transactions server-side.
2. **Immutable pricing revisions and quotes.** `feature_pricing_current` points to an append-only `feature_pricing_revisions` row. A revision freezes status, payer, billing model, currency, display name, description, marketing copy, time-based and finite-assessment trial counts, pack definitions, and Stripe product/price IDs. Admin save supplies the expected active revision; one compare-and-swap creates and activates the new revision. Stripe Product/Price operations use revision-scoped idempotency keys and metadata. A price change creates a new Price and updates the product default; old Prices are made inactive, without rewriting existing subscriptions.
3. **Checkout and funding receipts bind to that revision.** A checkout-attempt row snapshots revision ID, payer role/user, selected pack size/quantity, exact Stripe price ID, currency and quoted amount before contacting Stripe. It retains the idempotency key and any returned Session binding; uncertain creates remain reserved and must be reconciled before another Checkout can start. A unique verified payment receipt snapshots Stripe session/invoice/payment-intent IDs, amount/currency, quantity/credits and revision. Late or duplicate events cannot change the saved quote or mint credits twice. Unmatched events may retry under the same verified event identity after their exact provider-object mapping becomes available; terminal applied/blocked events remain deduped, while a reused event ID with different identity is rejected. Admin payer/price changes do not rewrite pending or paid records.
4. **Stripe event dispatch and reconciliation.** After signature verification, route every event through the Form Coach namespace resolver before generic handlers. It recognizes stable metadata and known feature object-ID mappings, including subscription, invoice, checkout, and refund events; unrecognized events continue to the existing dispatcher. For subscription state, retrieve current Stripe state, validate customer/user/feature/revision/price binding, and use database `state_revision`/event CAS so an old or duplicate event cannot overwrite a newer state. A stale CAS may retry with the same event identity only after fetching current provider state and supplying the new expected revision. Use a unique processed-event ledger. Do not trust event delivery order or `created` timestamps alone. No change may swallow current platform membership, coach membership, Armory purchase/refund or Connect events.
5. **Feature/payer access.** Initial config is `pricing_pending`, payer `coach`, no price, and no Stripe objects. `disabled` is a global stop for new sends and attempts, including for previously funded bundles; it preserves read-only history, exports, funding records, and immediate owner clip deletion. `pricing_pending` blocks new bundles/full use except explicit comp grants, while preserving already-funded sent assessments through their due dates. A funded sent assessment remains usable through its due date despite later cancellation, refund, expiry, or zero remaining credits. When pricing becomes `live` again, existing subscription contracts and unused credit-pack lots may fund new bundles under their original terms, even if the active billing model has changed; pending does not cancel, migrate, or reprice them. Coach-funded bundles do not require the recipient to hold a personal Form Coach entitlement. New member activity continues to use the existing `requireAccount()` platform-membership gate. Practice inside an open assessment is free.
6. **Payer/config freeze at send.** Drafts use the current pricing revision only when sent. `send_form_assessment` freezes the payer, config revision, quote/credits, and funding obligation on that assessment. An unfunded member-payer bundle stays member-payer if an administrator later changes the payer; an already coach-funded bundle never charges the member. No funding obligation transfers after send. A payer change applies only to later sends.
7. **Credit and refund rules.** A credit is consumed only for a per-assessment charge, a finite assessment-trial unit, or a credit-pack unit. An active subscription, comp grant, or time-based trial-days entitlement funds a bundle through an immutable funding receipt and does not decrement a credit balance. Existing contracts and unused packs remain stored through billing-model changes and may fund new bundles again once pricing is live. Select sources deterministically: explicit comp, active subscription (latest paid-through period, then stable subscription ID), time-based trial (earliest expiry, then stable grant ID), finite assessment-trial unit (earliest expiry, then stable lot ID), then purchased pack credit (earliest expiry, then stable pack-lot ID). `trialAssessments` is an immutable count on each pricing revision; its initial value is zero and no account receives trial credits from the seed. Coach payer records funding at send; member payer records it at first assessed attempt. A retry or reopening of the same assessment bundle within its authorized retry window keeps its original funding receipt and never debits again. Only a coach's explicit creation of a new assessment generation can create a new charge. Keep each purchased pack in a separate lot so a refund removes only that pack's unused units, not other packs, trials, subscriptions, or already-funded bundles. Default refund assumption: remove unused credits from the refunded pack and preserve already-funded sent bundles through their due date while the feature is enabled; no negative balance, credit debt, or hidden recharge.
8. **Account deletion is a billing fence.** The existing `platform_membership_deletion_locks` row has no profile FK and is a persistent tombstone. Account deletion already creates it before platform billing cancellation. Form Coach reservation RPCs and event handlers must check it before creating/binding checkout or granting access. Deletion reconciles and expires all known/uncertain Form Coach checkouts, cancels exact verified subscriptions, and refuses to finish while a Stripe create is unresolved. A late event after the lock cancels any verified late subscription and never recreates an entitlement. Feature-owned storage is also removed during deletion. The tombstone remains after the profile is gone.
9. **Attempts reserve slots atomically.** Before an assessed attempt starts, an RPC locks the item and creates/reuses a stable reservation keyed by an idempotency key. Running reservations count toward the configured maximum of three, not just submitted attempts. A lease and heartbeat permit recovery of a stalled session without opening a second slot or charging again. The same reservation ID is resumed after lease expiry; finalized results are immutable. `finish_attempt` is idempotent for the same result digest and rejects attempts to overwrite a finished score.
10. **Completion and outbox.** Existing free programs stay dynamic. Completion is true only when the current required-day set is nonempty and every current day has a matching completion. A database guard rejects member-supplied non-null `completed_at` on INSERT and UPDATE; only the trusted recompute transition can set it. Undoing a day or editing current program days may change completion state. Auto-send dedupes by a stable member/program/rule-generation identity across undo/recomplete and leave/re-enroll; a coach explicitly creates a new generation for an intentional resend. The separate paid-program contract must own enrollment start dates server-side, without changing existing free-program timing. The trigger only writes/reuses a durable outbox row, never charges or calls Stripe. The job revalidates enrollment, current completion, rule state/ownership, template publication and entitlement before debit. Cancelled/ineligible rows are suppressed without charge; a missing credit leaves completion intact and the outbox pending with a coach paywall signal. Paid program versions are out of scope until their owner merges its contract.
11. **Eligibility and verdict.** The standing dumbbell curl registry has `standing` and limb-loading tags. Before session start, use only normalized member-owned exercise exclusions/clearance flags from the intake interface. No intake record means “no known exclusion”; it is not an intake-service outage and does not force unrelated members to fill a health form. A lookup/service failure is handled separately with a safe retry/help message. Do not show raw notes/answers to the coach or send any health data to the LLM. Client-reported scores are untrusted; only the coach's explicit Learned/Needs work verdict is authoritative.
12. **Privacy and network.** Pose processing uses MediaPipe on-device; raw frames and landmarks never leave browser memory or enter analytics/storage/LLM payloads. Coach reference videos and cue audio are coach uploads; a member clip is uploaded only after per-attempt opt-in, with default off, ≤60 seconds, and is immediately deletable by its owner at any time. That delete route is authenticated but independent of feature status, credits, base membership, and coach consent. References/cue assets are private and immutable after publication; signed URLs expire within one hour. Default clip retention is 90 days and configurable. Network policy covers coach reference processing, member session pages, and Worker contexts. It allows local app assets and only the configured Storage origin when an explicit asset/clip upload needs it; block unapproved external telemetry and test each context.
13. **MediaPipe telemetry and runtime are version-specific.** On 2026-11-19, official npm metadata listed stable `@mediapipe/tasks-vision` 1.2.3 (Apache-2.0); its privacy text and Google's privacy notice distinguish locally processed image/video inputs from performance/utilization metrics sent to Google. A prior Google maintainer response about an older web SDK is not evidence for 1.2.3. Phase 2 must inspect the exact pinned package, test actual browser/Worker requests and bodies, and verify whether telemetry can be disabled. Block external connections by default; if required inference cannot operate under the approved policy, pause for owner review instead of silently enabling telemetry. Never claim all traffic is local before that audit.
14. **Media asset/upload limits.** Self-host the exact pinned WASM/model assets, record checksums and notices, and do not use a runtime CDN. The requested 200 MB reference-video maximum is unverified against the current hosted Supabase Storage global/project-plan cap and deployment limits. Before selecting an app cap, verify the existing configured cap without changing provider plans; do not claim or silently upgrade to 200 MB. Until verified, Phase 3 will use a conservative limit no higher than the hosted cap and display it accurately.
15. **LLM selection.** Only `FORM_COACH_LLM_PROVIDER`, `FORM_COACH_LLM_MODEL`, and `FORM_COACH_LLM_API_KEY` choose a provider/model/key. Missing/unknown values fail closed; no automatic AI Gateway route or model substitution. Future AI code follows the Vercel AI SDK skill and version-matched installed docs. Provider packages are added only for a specifically selected configured provider, and all LLM tests are mocked.
16. **Other worktrees.** On main `0a796b4`, PR77 program intro and PR78/PR79 for Intake and Dashboard/DailyFuel are merged. The hosted migration history contains 56 rows through `20261118151000`; Intake's database changes are already applied. The Program foundation `155000` remains isolated and unreleased, so do not copy it to production. Wait for root integration before importing these APIs or updating shared pages. Reserve Form Coach migrations at `20261118160000` and later. Root owns commits, merges, production and acceptance.

### Phase 1A — Pricing and billing data foundation (approved bounded scope)

**Outcome:** Revisioned feature pricing and immutable billing/accounting records, with pure pricing/access models. This is only the foundation: no pricing UI, Checkout/server adapter, webhook route wiring, shared deletion/export callsites, navigation, or localization is included. Do not describe Phase 1 as complete after Phase 1A.

**Only allowed files in this phase:**

- `supabase/migrations/20261118160000_feature_pricing_billing.sql` — revisioned pricing config with the initial `form_coach` row at `pricing_pending`/coach payer and no Stripe Price/Product IDs; immutable display/description/marketing fields and finite assessment-trial count; immutable quote, pack, funding-receipt, subscription mapping, event-deduplication and credit-ledger records; explicit comp-grant history; service-only compare-and-swap/reservation/receipt/refund/credit primitives; deletion-tombstone checks against the existing `platform_membership_deletion_locks`; owner-safe read projections/RLS. Do not add arbitrary browser debit or caller-asserted paid-access APIs. No account is auto-comped and no credits are granted by the seed.
- `supabase/tests/form_coach_billing_test.sql` — pgTAP coverage for defaults, admin/member access boundaries, revision/quote immutability, compare-and-swap, idempotent receipt/credit operations, pack quantity, unused-only refunds without debt, feature namespace mappings, and deletion tombstone handling of uncertain/late states. Run only on a new disposable clone after root approves its exact name, baseline/dependencies, and final migration SHA.
- `src/lib/form-coach/types.ts`, `src/lib/form-coach/pricing.ts`, and `src/lib/form-coach/pricing.test.ts` — domain types and pure validation/pricing/access models. No provider, database, browser, or secret access.

**Phase 1A acceptance and boundary:**

- Initial persisted config is exactly `pricing_pending`, coach payer, no price/Stripe objects, and zero assessment-trial grants. Revisioned display/description/marketing fields, billing settings and trial counts are immutable; admin compare-and-swap cannot overwrite a concurrent revision. No implicit comp, account fixture, or credit grant.
- Quote and funding metadata bind immutably to feature/config revision, payer identity and payer role, pack quantity/units, amount/currency, and provider-object identifiers where later created. Unknown provider creates remain reserved for later reconciliation; this phase creates no provider objects.
- Session binding may enrich only previously missing customer/subscription IDs; a known ID cannot be changed, and a conflicting late ID is rejected. Customer ownership is scoped to feature and payer, so the same payer can reuse one customer across price/config revisions; each checkout and subscription still keeps its own immutable revision/price quote. Subscription payment stays retryable until both customer and subscription bindings are known, and the payment transaction cannot report paid without its receipt and reconciliation row. A late verified binding repairs a legacy paid receipt's missing subscription row without rewriting its receipt.
- At most one nonterminal/collectible subscription may exist per feature and payer. Reservation checks run under the payer lock and a partial unique index is the database backstop. A new subscription checkout is blocked through pending reconciliation, active/trialing, and other potentially collectible states; a verified terminal cancellation or expired-incomplete state releases the slot. Repeated per-assessment pack purchases remain permitted.
- Service-only receipts and operations are idempotent. A verified receipt maps to exactly its quoted pack, once. Refund application removes only unused credits; it cannot create negative debt, double-debit, or erase already-funded bundle history. Namespace mappings distinguish `form_coach` from generic memberships/Armory. The bundle due deadline is validated before selecting any new funding source; an expired immutable receipt stays expired and cannot fall through to another source.
- Admin pricing compare-and-swap requires a positive supported expected revision; null, nonpositive, overflow-boundary, and stale values cannot change the current pointer or append history.
- Paid access is derived only from persisted, server-verified records and explicit comp history, never a client-supplied receipt, pin, status, payer, or amount. Member clients cannot create/update grants, receipts, entitlements, or ledger rows. Owner projections omit processor secrets/identifiers.
- `disabled` blocks new sends/attempts even for previously funded bundles; when the status is not disabled, a sent funded bundle remains usable through its due date despite later cancellation, refund, or zero balance. `pricing_pending` blocks new use except comp, but does not cancel old contracts; if pricing becomes live again, grandfathered subscription and pack contracts retain their original terms. Drafts take the active payer/config only at send; payer and funding obligation are then frozen. An unfunded member-payer send remains member-funded after an admin payer switch; a coach-funded send never charges the member.
- The existing persistent account-deletion tombstone fences new reservation, unknown/uncertain checkout binding, and late receipts/access. It remains after profile deletion. Billing rows survive account deletion where needed for reconciliation; the persistent tombstone blocks any late grant. Phase 1A adds the database primitives and tests only; shared deletion/export callsites and provider reconciliation workers are deferred.
- All tests use pure inputs or synthetic pgTAP fixtures. No Stripe object/session, LLM request, hosted database write, provider operation, real account comp, camera, or media upload is created.

**Phase 1B — deferred integration, not authorized in this task:** Admin pricing screen/actions; Checkout and Stripe adapters; namespaced webhook dispatch before generic handlers; account-deletion/data-export callsites; billing/paywall page; navigation and English/Spanish labels. Phase 1B must be separately reviewed and integrated after the Program worker’s shared financial-file changes.
